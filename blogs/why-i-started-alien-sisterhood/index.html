Why I Started Alien Sisterhood: Where Human Stories Meet the Cosmos

If you’ve ever felt like an outsider or even an alien, like you didn’t quite belong on this planet, then you already understand the heart of Alien Sisterhood.

I started this independently owned online studio because I needed to tell stories that felt raw, surreal, and human. Growing up in a small town in southern Japan, I escaped into my sketchbooks, creating new worlds, languages, and religions just to survive and daydream about the world I wanted to live in.

Years later, after heartbreaks and starting over, I picked up the pen again. And Alien Sisterhood was born.

What is Alien Sisterhood?

Alien Sisterhood is more than just an art studio. It’s a universe of cosmic artwork that mixes raw human experiences with storytelling. Through alien girls, colorful visuals, and dreamlike pastel worlds, I explore what it means to survive, transform, and find your people/place in this world.

If you crave art that’s both beautiful and brutally honest, this space is for you.

Why illustrated lyric essays?

Illustrated lyric essays allow me to merge words and visuals into something powerful. Each page is a portal; art you can feel, paired with stories you can see yourself inside. These stories are grounded in real experience and emotions of love, loss, survival, and self-discovery.

Behind the Scenes

Through my blogs, I share the process. Raw sketches, worldbuilding notes, and podcasts where I talk openly about life, creativity, and the realities of being an artist. It’s not polished, but it is real.

Work With Me

Alien Sisterhood is for collaborators, dreamers, and visionaries who want to bring new worlds to life. If you’re a writer, animator, musician, filmmaker, or creative soul who wants to collaborate, let’s connect. Together, we can build art that transcends creative norms.

Join the Journey

If any of this resonates with you, I invite you to: Collaborate → Contact me today!

This is just the beginning.
Alien Sisterhood is where human stories meet the cosmos, and I’d love for you to be part of it.
