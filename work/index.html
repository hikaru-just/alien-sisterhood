Welcome to the world of Alien Sisterhood

My illustrated essays have their own universe — Trash Moon Diaries — plus memoir-like essays from “A Rant Sesh with an Asian Bish.” As an author, I believe in challenging the boundaries of what’s acceptable to the mainstream audience. I write stories of women navigating this world, hoping that it will create a safe space for them to accept themselves and just be.

Trash Moon Diaries

A compilation of unique stories of three alien girls in their late 20s. They fumble, laugh, and transform together under broken streetlights in a city that resembles LA. These stories are built on the premise that life's most beautiful moments often grow from what we are taught to discard and leave behind.

Are you ready to embrace the chaos, the heartbreak, and the weird?
